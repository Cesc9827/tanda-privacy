Controller and contact

Cesc Maynés is responsible for Tanda. For privacy questions, requests about your data or support, you can write to the contact address shown on this page.

Data and purposes

The app stores the habits you create, their names and icons, goals, schedules, activity days, reminders, pauses, progress history and preferences to provide the features you choose to use.

Storage and third parties

Habit and profile data is stored locally using iOS. The app and its widgets share the information they need in a protected container on your device. Tanda does not send us your history, has no data-collection server, includes no advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.

iOS may include data in backups depending on your settings. Those backups and system services are managed through your device and their providers’ terms.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications that you configure and that require iOS permission. You can change them in Tanda or manage permission in system settings. Habit names and progress may appear in widgets and notifications, including on a locked device, depending on your settings.

Email support

The contact option opens your email app with our address and a subject that includes the Tanda version. A message is sent only if you choose to send it. We process the address and content you send to handle your request, on the basis of that request and our legitimate interest in providing support. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not necessary.

The sender’s and recipient’s email providers, including Gmail, are involved. Their processing is governed by their own policies, including their terms on international transfers. We do not use your messages for advertising or commercial profiling.

Message retention

We retain support messages for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and comply with applicable legal obligations. When that need ends, they are deleted. You can request information or deletion by writing to the contact shown, subject to any data we must retain under a legal obligation.

Deletion and permissions

Local data remains until you delete it. “Delete all data” removes your habits, history, reminders and widget data, clears your preferences and returns to the welcome screen. If deletion cannot be completed, Tanda shows a message so you can retry.

iOS permissions, such as notification permission, are managed in system settings and are not reset when Tanda’s data is deleted. This action also does not delete backups or email messages: you manage those in the relevant services.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability for the data we process by writing to our contact. Data held only on your device is not accessible to us. We do not make automated decisions with legal effects on you. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or another competent authority.

Security and changes

Tanda uses iOS app isolation and storage mechanisms. No system can promise absolute security. This policy will be updated if features or data processing change. The date on this page identifies the version you are reading.

This policy’s website

If you visit the web version of this policy, the hosting provider may process technical connection data to serve and protect the page. The provider and its privacy policy are identified on that web page. The copy included in Tanda can be read offline and does not require visiting that website.
